The idea behind In Common

DIFFERENT
PEOPLE.
COMMON
GROUND.

Finding your people shouldn't feel complicated. In Common is being created as a space where connecting feels more natural, comfortable, and genuine.

Designed for adults diagnosed with or who identify with Asperger's, our online social club brings people together through shared interests, conversations, and community.

No medical documentation required. No pressure to socialize a certain way. Just the opportunity to meet people who might see the world a little like you do.
